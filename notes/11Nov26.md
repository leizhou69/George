## 2. Why Fable 5.2 scored 0

- The catalog `Source` column had the value `KnownDiseaseAssociatedLoci` for every given pathogenic locus plus a few unlabelled loci: 10 extras in 5'UTR and 3 in CDS. All withheld keys were among those extras (in CDS the extras were exactly the keys).
- Fable 5.2 noticed this in both runs, refused to use `Source` as a feature, recognised the extra loci as established disease genes, and excluded them (and close neighbours) from its top 50.
- Its own score would have ranked the excluded keys at 3 and 4 of 6,633 (5'UTR) and 4, 106 and 372 of 34,863 (CDS).
- Gemini also noticed the label in its CDS run but left the loci in its list.

### Why the CDS lists changed for Opus 4.8 and Sonnet 5

Both causes were tested by re-fitting each November model from the feature table that run saved.

- **Opus 4.8, CDS (1 of 50 shared):** the November score has no repeat-length term. July controlled for length and included log length; November controlled for GC content and scored on AlphaGenome features alone, giving short-repeat candidates (median 4 units versus 14). Adding log length to the November model raises overlap with July from 3 to 18 and key recovery from 0/3 to 2/3.
- **Sonnet 5, CDS (6 of 50 shared):** same ingredients, different scoring form. July used a hand-weighted sum of rank percentiles; November fitted a logistic regression on z-scored features, which two artefacts dominated:
  - impurity: 99.4% of loci have purity 1.0, so any impure locus becomes extreme, and the signal comes largely from the 10 `CNG` positives (purity capped at 0.67);
  - Illumina variability: present for 1 of 28 positives, missing values set to 0, so the model penalises every locus that has a value.
  Dropping those two features raises overlap with July from 6 to 18; applying July's rank-percentile form to November's features gives 25.
- **Not the cause:** the re-mask (neither model used the dropped columns in July), run effort, the prompt, or the backend.
- Sonnet 5's 2/3 CDS recovery in November is partly a product of the impurity weighting, so it is not evidence that the November approach is better.
