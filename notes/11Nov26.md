## 2. Why Fable 5.2 scored 0

- The catalog `Source` column had the value `KnownDiseaseAssociatedLoci` for every given pathogenic locus plus a few unlabelled loci: 10 extras in 5'UTR and 3 in CDS. All withheld keys were among those extras (in CDS the extras were exactly the keys).
- Fable 5.2 noticed this in both runs, refused to use `Source` as a feature, recognised the extra loci as established disease genes, and excluded them (and close neighbours) from its top 50.
- Its own score would have ranked the excluded keys at 3 and 4 of 6,633 (5'UTR) and 4, 106 and 372 of 34,863 (CDS).
- Gemini also noticed the label in its CDS run but left the loci in its list.

## 6. Sonnet 5 and Opus 4.8: July versus November

| Run | July time | Nov time | Top-50 loci shared | Keys (Jul → Nov) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sonnet 5, 5'UTR | 3 h 36 m (16 min real work) | 14 min | 18 | 2/2 → 2/2 |
| Opus 4.8, 5'UTR | 7 min | 7 min | 30 | 1/2 → 2/2 |
| Sonnet 5, CDS | 22 min | 12 min | 6 | 0/3 → 2/3 |
| Opus 4.8, CDS | 6 min | 6 min | 1 | 1/3 → 0/3 |

- The July Sonnet 5 5'UTR run was long only because one `find /` shell command hung until the 12,000 s execution timeout.
- **Sonnet 5, 5'UTR:** the July run predates the GCN scope block (scored all 10,053 loci; 8 non-GCN candidates). November scored the 6,650 GCN loci and added contact-map features to the score.
- **Opus 4.8, 5'UTR:** same method both times (contact-map dose-response plus length and polymorphism); the most stable pair.
- **Sonnet 5, CDS:** similar ingredients, but November's list is dominated by loci from the T2T-polymorphic source catalog; the driving feature was not pinned down.
- **Opus 4.8, CDS:** July controlled for repeat length; November controlled for GC content instead and scored on AlphaGenome features with no length term, giving short-repeat candidates.
- The re-mask did not cause these differences: none of the four July runs used either dropped column in its analysis.
- Not ruled out: a server-side change to the models behind the same model IDs.
